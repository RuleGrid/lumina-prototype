Six thousand years of practice

Same grape. New vessel.

Keep scrolling. The story moves with you.

  1. 4000 BC

    The qvevri

    Georgia buries clay vessels of fermenting grapes. Wine is born underground.

  2. Antiquity

    The amphora

    Greeks and Romans pour wine by the sea, at feasts, at games. Wine is social.

  3. 1600s

    The bottle

    Glass and cork lock wine into cellars, rituals, and a vocabulary of its own.

  4. Now

    The can

    Same craft, same land, no corkscrew. Wine goes back to where people are.
